Privacy Policy Support

1. Overview

MigraineTrack is designed so that your health data never leaves your device. We do not operate any servers, we have no user accounts, and we do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

2. Data stored on your device

All of the following information is stored locally on your iPhone / Apple Watch and is under your sole control:

  • Migraine attacks, intensity, symptoms, triggers, and medications you record
  • Barometric pressure and weather context captured with each attack
  • Sleep and menstrual-cycle data read from Apple Health, used only for on-device correlation analysis
  • Your app preferences (language, theme, etc.)

3. iCloud backup

Optional backup and restore uses your own iCloud account. The data travels through Apple's iCloud infrastructure under Apple's privacy terms. The developer has no ability to access, view, or restore your iCloud data.

4. Apple Health (HealthKit)

MigraineTrack reads sleep and menstrual-cycle samples from Apple Health solely to show on-device correlations with your migraines. In accordance with Apple's HealthKit developer requirements, this data is never used for advertising, data mining, or sharing with third parties, and is never uploaded anywhere.

5. Location & barometer

Location (when-in-use) is requested only to fetch local weather context for your records. It is processed on-device and never stored on our side — we have no servers to store it on. The device barometer is read locally and saved only inside your own records.

6. Subscriptions

The optional Pro subscription is billed and processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles all payment information; the developer never sees your payment details. Purchase records exist only within your Apple ID and the App Store. By using the app you agree to Apple's standard Terms of Use (EULA).

7. No tracking of any kind

  • No analytics SDKs
  • No advertising SDKs
  • No third-party trackers or cookies
  • No crash reporters that transmit data off-device

8. No medical advice

MigraineTrack is a self-tracking tool for personal record keeping. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and is not a substitute for consultation with a qualified healthcare professional. Always seek the advice of a physician with any questions regarding migraines, medications, or your health.

9. Children's privacy

MigraineTrack does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone — including children under 13 — because no data is collected at all.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date, and in-app where applicable.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/hairuizhi-cn/migrainetrack-site.
